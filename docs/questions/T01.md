# T01 questions (repair cycle 1): no contact setting meets the flatness acceptance

Stopped per the repair brief. Evidence: `python -m cs182_icl.sim.contact_diag` (src/cs182_icl/sim/contact_diag.py,
about 6 s). Full table is in docs/handoffs/T01.md section 5; key rows below (5 corner cases; selection only on flatness).

## Diagnosis (measured)
- Force is a world-frame force at the COM (xipos == xpos), xfrc_applied torque = 0, push height consistent.
- Settled state is fine (4 contacts, penetration 1e-5 m, height error 10 um).
- m=0.5, mu=0.6, F=26, step 0 of the push: sum of normal forces 15.05 N vs m g = 4.91 N, T/N = 0.600 exactly
  (Coulomb holds), vz = +0.020 m/s after one step -> cube leaves the floor (ncon = 0 in following steps).
- The same hop occurs with NO applied force: cube sliding at 3 m/s decelerating under friction alone rises 3.05 mm and
  loses contact in 136 of 150 steps. So it is sliding-friction/normal coupling, not the push onset or force step.
- Mechanism (hand derivation + consistent with data): MuJoCo's soft constraint solves a cone-constrained QP; the
  friction rows have reference acceleration -b*v_t with b = 2/(dmax*timeconst) (100 1/s here), demanding a tangential
  force m*b*v >> mu*N. Projection onto the cone raises N by about mu*(demand)/(1+mu^2), so a fast slider is pushed upward
  in proportion to speed (13 m/s in the light cases). Rigid-body theory has no such effect (needs mu > 1 to tip).

## Candidates (all fail; max over the 5 corner cases)
Every geom solref/solimp (stiffer, softer, direct, high damping, impedance clamp 0.9999), margin/gap, impratio 0.1..1000,
pyramidal cone (+noslip), noslip, iterations/tolerance, PGS/CG, no warmstart: maxrot 179.8-180 deg and ncon=0, or the
cube cannot settle within 1e-4 m / 1e-3 m/s (margin>0 shifts the rest height by 2 mm; gap has no effect in this MuJoCo 4.0.1).
Best flatness: pair-level `solreffriction` (not a listed knob, contact pair needed): timeconst 0.2 -> 1.9 deg, 2.6 mm
excursion; 1 -> 0.7 deg, 1.4 mm, but it caps friction at ~m*b_f*v (viscous at speed) so d errs by 7% to 1000% vs
analytic. It removes the Coulomb behaviour the experiment is about, and still has ncon = 0 steps (except tc >= 1 on the slide test).
So flatness and a Coulomb friction law are not simultaneously achievable with MuJoCo soft contact at dt = 0.001.

## Options (human decision; PROTOCOL 4 / DECISIONS)
A. Translation-only scene (slide joints x,y; no rotation/hop). Rotation is identically 0, so the max-rotation flag is
   vacuous (always 0) and the "rotation within 1 deg" filter loses meaning; the Coulomb sliding is then exactly what the
   solver gives (a 1-DoF-like contact with N set by the z constraint; check d against analytic). Changes PROTOCOL 4 (cube is a free body).
B. Keep the free cube, accept hopping, and redefine the validity rule: report d for all rollouts, treat the rotation flag as
   the real indicator (910 of 1000 training-range pushes exceed 1 deg, 85 exceed 45 deg per the review) -> a large part of the
   data would be flagged; not recommended.
C. Free cube with a vertical slide joint pinning z only (hop impossible, pitch still possible) or a hinge-locked pitch: a
   middle path, also a PROTOCOL 4 change; I did not test it.
Recommendation: A, then re-validate d against the analytic formula (T02). Nothing was changed in cube.xml.
