## 3. Deviations from PROTOCOL/INTERFACES
- Scene is an IDEALIZED 1D implementation of the declared law (N = m*g), not contact physics. max_rotation_deg is vacuous (always 0.0).
- Friction is an explicit Coulomb force, NOT MuJoCo frictionloss. frictionloss (default solref/solimp) was tried first and creeps below
  threshold: m=1, mu=0.45 (threshold 4.41 N), F=4.0/4.3/4.4 moved the cube 0.63/0.68/0.70 mm (scripts/sim_validate.py last line), violating stiction.
- Stick rule: v_free = v + dt F/m; if |v_free| <= dt mu g then qvel=0 and net force exactly 0. Settling is trivial (initial state exactly at rest).
- Stopping rule linear-only (|v|<1e-4 m/s for 50 steps; angular condition removed, no rotation DoF); cap 15 s; timeouts returned.

## 5. Known weaknesses
- Sim-vs-analytic agreement (1e-5 rel) can show only that the code implements the ideal law with small discretisation error (explicit Euler,
  O(dt)), that dt/stiction/T^2 behave, and that API/batch machinery works. It CANNOT show that real pushes obey N = m*g, Coulomb friction,
  or any contact physics; SUMMARY must say so. Realistic contact is a later relaxation.
- Because the sim is nearly exactly the closed form, E1 comparisons against the analytic estimator are not an independent test.
