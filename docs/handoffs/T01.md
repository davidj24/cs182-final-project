## 3. Deviations from PROTOCOL/INTERFACES
- assets/cube.xml sets `solimp="0.99 0.999 0.001"` on both geoms. With MuJoCo default solimp the settled cube sits 0.108 mm low (mass independent), failing the G1 height tolerance 1e-4 m; with it the sag is 0.010 mm. Effect on d: <= ~1e-4 relative at moving cases tested vs a 0.999 variant; T02 must confirm in its dt/analytic validation (human decides whether to accept).
- t_stop is the time the 50-step dwell completes (rule fires), i.e. about 0.05 s after rest; stop rule is checked only after the force ends.
- Settling failure raises RuntimeError (broken physics stops data generation); rotation/timeouts are returned as flags.
- simulate_batch uses a fork pool (no `__main__` guard needed); results independent of workers/seed (seed only fixes job layout).
