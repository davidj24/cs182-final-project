dt rerun: `simulate_push(cube, action_for_dt(a, 0.0005), dt=0.0005)` (2k steps, 100-step dwell). Analytic comparison must use T = k_orig*DT, not Action.T of the rescaled action (documented in rollout.py).

## 3. Deviations from PROTOCOL/INTERFACES
- assets/cube.xml sets `solimp="0.99 0.999 0.001"` on both geoms. With MuJoCo default solimp the settled cube sits 0.108 mm low (mass independent), failing the G1 height tolerance 1e-4 m; with it the sag is 0.010 mm. Effect on d: <= ~1e-4 relative at moving cases tested vs a 0.999 variant; T02 must confirm in its dt/analytic validation (human decides whether to accept).
- t_stop is the time the 50-step dwell completes (rule fires), i.e. about 0.05 s after rest; stop rule is checked only after the force ends.
- Settling failure raises RuntimeError (broken physics stops data generation); rotation/timeouts are returned as flags.
- simulate_batch uses a fork pool (no `__main__` guard needed); results independent of workers/seed (seed only fixes job layout).

## 5. Known weaknesses / surprises
- Real rotation: pushing at the centre of mass with friction at the base pitches the cube. Max rotation: 8.76 deg (m=0.5, mu=0.3, F=26, k=200), 5.74 deg (middle case), 1.83 deg (heavy/grippy). Many training-range rollouts will exceed the 1 deg flag; counts are for T02/T04 to report. d still matches analytic closely in these cases (e.g. 17.336 vs 17.3358 m; middle 0.7946 vs 0.7944).
- Rest-after-push ring-down makes t_stop larger than ideal (middle: 0.867 s vs ideal 0.68+0.05).
- Plane renders dark in frames. Creep, timeouts and dt=0.0005 only spot-tested (not statistics; T02).
