## 3. Deviations from PROTOCOL/INTERFACES
- physics_fit_predict gained keyword-only options (force_sep_min, move_eps, fit_space); the
  positional signature (context, query) is unchanged.
- Rules I chose (documented in the module docstring): force span among moving pushes >= 3 N
  and >= 2 moving pushes for a line; moving := d_obs > 1e-6 m; "query force observed" = equal
  up to rel 1e-9 (float storage only). Same-force pooled h includes clipped pushes at F_q
  (so clipped at F_q predicts 0). Same-force rule takes precedence over the line fit.
- Repeated measurement_id with a different action/value raises ValueError.

## 4. Open questions
- Fit space: the control message says "OLS on d/(F T^2) = A F - B"; PROTOCOL §8, the T03
  brief and the T00 docstring say least squares of d on (F^2 T^2, -F T^2). I implemented
  the PROTOCOL one as the default and the line form as fit_space="line". They agree exactly
  on analytic data but weight MuJoCo discrepancy differently (d-space weights long slides
  heavily). Control should pick one before E1.
- With the 3 N span rule and F ~ U[13, 26], about 41% of E1 c=2 items will be
  not_determined (probability that two uniform forces lie within 3 N: 1-(10/13)^2).
  That is expected behavior, but T06 should report coverage per c.

## 5. Known weaknesses

- Unphysical fits (A or B <= 0) are returned with status "ok" and flagged in reason only.
